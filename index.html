Cross-Contamination Free Strategy

How we keep customers with severe food allergies 100% safe.

1

Fresh-to-Freeze Line

We purchase fresh strawberries, bananas, mangoes, pineapples, and raspberries from quality produce aisles. They are washed, hand-chopped, and flash-frozen on flat trays before sorting into labeled containers.

2

Strict Sanitization

Every blender, pitcher, and utensil is thoroughly washed and sanitized between every order to maintain absolute safety standards.

The Overturned Rule

"Because nothing is mixed together in a factory bag, your ingredients NEVER touch other cross-contaminants. Our stand is 100% allergy safe!"
